# Merchantiva — Brand & Voice Guidelines

## One-line Positioning
Merchantiva is the decision and implementation partner for ecommerce entrepreneurs — from first idea to eventual sale. Merchantiva helps entrepreneurs figure out what to do, then helps them do it.

## What Merchantiva Is
- A decision-support partner across the full ecommerce entrepreneur lifecycle
- Research-and-intelligence led
- An integrated system: intelligence + education + community + implementation

## What Merchantiva Is NOT (avoid this language/framing)
- "Another ecommerce agency"
- "An online course platform"
- A one-off service shop with a menu of disconnected offerings

## Core Message to Repeat
> What is the next best decision for this entrepreneur, right now?
Every page, CTA, and piece of content should trace back to helping the visitor make their next decision — not just "buy this service."

## Voice
- Direct, credible, research-grounded — not hype-y or "guru" marketing language
- Speaks to entrepreneurs as capable adults making real business decisions, not beginners needing hand-holding
- Confident but not overpromising ("reduce risk," "reduce uncertainty" — not "guaranteed success")

## Terminology to Use Consistently
- "Ecommerce entrepreneur" (the customer)
- "Lifecycle stage" (Discover, Validate, Launch, Acquire Customers, Scale, Expand, Exit)
- "Opportunity report," "validation package" (named research products — don't rename ad hoc)
- "Membership" (not "subscription" unless that becomes the finalized commercial term)

## Brand Personality
Merchantiva should feel: Strategic, Intelligent, Research-driven, Practical, Trustworthy, Modern, Entrepreneurial.

## Brand Character
Merchantiva should **not** feel like: a generic ecommerce agency, a dropshipping course, a marketing influencer brand, or a freelancer marketplace.

Merchantiva **should** feel like: a strategy firm, a research company, a technology-enabled partner.

## Visual Identity System
The Merchantiva visual system should communicate intelligence, trust, structure, and strategic decision-making. The brand should resemble a research and technology company rather than a traditional ecommerce service provider.

### Color System

| Role | Name | Hex | Purpose | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Navy | `#0B0F14` | Intelligence, trust, stability, strategic thinking | Main backgrounds, brand foundation, primary visual elements |
| Secondary | Dark Slate | `#2A3441` | Depth and structure, modern tech aesthetic | Cards, UI components, secondary backgrounds |
| Neutral | Cool Gray | `#667182` | Balance, readability, professional research feel | Supporting text, data displays, secondary information |
| Accent | Electric Cyan | `#4DD6FF` | Intelligence, innovation, technology, forward movement | CTAs, highlights, interactive elements, data visualization |

### Typography Direction
The typography system should communicate precision, clarity, professionalism, and modern technology:
- Clean sans-serif fonts
- Strong hierarchy
- High readability
- Professional presentation

Should work consistently across: website, research reports, presentations, dashboards, educational resources.

### Logo System
**Status: Locked (Aug 2026)**

**Primary lockup** — serif "M" monogram (with a small geometric negative-space cut at center, echoing the brand's decision-point/precision motif) + tracked-out sans-serif wordmark "MERCHANTIVA" + tagline "BETTER DECISIONS. BETTER BUSINESSES." Use for the website header, business cards, presentation covers, and anywhere there's room for the full lockup.

**Icon / small-format mark** — a simplified, bolder standalone version of the M, closer to a solid geometric monogram (no serif detailing) for use as favicon, app icon, and social avatar. The full serif M with its finer details will not hold up below ~32px; the standalone icon needs heavier, simpler strokes to survive down to 16×16.

**Test before finalizing any mark:** shrink it to 24×24px. If it's still instantly recognizable, it passes. If it collapses into noise, simplify further.

**Icon files (tested at 200/64/32/16px — legible at every size):**
- `docs/brand-assets/icon-mark.svg` — the finalized mark, transparent background, `currentColor` fill. Use for inline embedding anywhere you need it to inherit surrounding text/theme color.
- `public/favicon.svg` — ready-to-use app icon: light rounded-square background (`#F5F7FA`), Deep Navy (`#0B0F14`) M. The diamond in the middle is negative space, not a separate shape — it only reads correctly against a light background, so don't drop this mark onto a dark tile without redesigning the fill logic.

**Avoid:** shopping carts, product boxes, generic ecommerce symbols, discount-oriented visuals, ornamental/heraldic crests (knotwork, coats of arms), literal ships/maritime scenes as the primary icon. These either misrepresent the "decision and implementation partner" positioning or fail the small-size legibility test.

### Tagline
**"Better decisions. Better businesses."**
Direct distillation of the guiding principle in `docs/strategy.md` ("What is the next best decision for this ecommerce entrepreneur?"). Use alongside the primary lockup; not required next to the standalone icon.

### Imagery Direction
**Use:** entrepreneurs building companies, data analysis, strategy sessions, ecommerce operations, technology systems.

**Avoid:** generic shopping imagery, product shelves, shipping boxes, consumer retail visuals.

### Design Principle
Every visual element should reinforce: **Merchantiva transforms ecommerce uncertainty into informed decisions.**

## Tone by Section
- **Home / top of funnel**: clear, welcoming, oriented around "where are you right now?"
- **Lifecycle pages**: practical and specific — answer the actual questions entrepreneurs are asking
- **Services/Membership**: transparent about what's included, no false urgency or scarcity tactics
- **About**: mission-driven but grounded — explain the *why* behind needs-based positioning briefly, don't over-explain

## Things to Avoid
- Fabricated statistics, testimonials, or "X,XXX entrepreneurs helped" claims without real numbers
- Generic ecommerce stock phrases ("unlock your potential," "take your business to the next level")
- Overloading a single page with all 6 business-model layers at once — let the lifecycle stage guide what's shown
