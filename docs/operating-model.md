# Merchantiva Operating Model

This is the missing "how" layer: `docs/strategy.md` explains why Merchantiva exists, `docs/lifecycle.md` explains who it serves and their needs, this doc explains how Merchantiva actually delivers value.

## Purpose
Merchantiva helps ecommerce entrepreneurs make better decisions and execute those decisions throughout the ecommerce lifecycle.

## Core System
Merchantiva operates through four connected engines.

### 1. Intelligence Engine
Creates the knowledge advantage.

**Outputs:** Product research, market research, competitor intelligence, supplier intelligence, ecommerce trends, case studies.

**Feeds:** Strategy, content, client recommendations, Academy.

### 2. Education Engine (Academy)
Turns knowledge into learning.

**Outputs:** Courses, training videos, templates, guides, community, workshops.

**Purpose:** Help entrepreneurs learn how to make better ecommerce decisions.

### 3. Implementation Engine
Turns decisions into action.

**Outputs:** Ecommerce startup services, store development, product launches, marketing systems, automation, analytics.

**Purpose:** Help entrepreneurs execute.

For the actual tools, automation, and roles behind this engine, see `docs/venture-operating-system.md` — Opportunity Discovery System and Ecommerce Build System.

### 4. Growth Engine
Helps businesses improve and scale.

**Outputs:** Customer acquisition, conversion optimization, automation, operations, expansion strategy, exit preparation.

For the actual tools, automation, and roles behind this engine, see `docs/venture-operating-system.md` — Marketing Automation System and Analytics Intelligence System.

The four engines are connected, not siloed: Intelligence feeds the other three; Implementation and Growth both draw on Intelligence and often hand off to each other as a business matures. See `docs/strategy.md` — Merchantiva Delivery Model — for how this plays out within a single customer engagement.

## Customer Journey System
A customer can enter at any stage, not just at the beginning. This maps onto the 5-stage customer lifecycle in `docs/lifecycle.md`:

| Entry Point | Maps to Lifecycle Stage | Need | Solutions |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Entrepreneur | Start Your Business | "What should I sell?" | Research → Validation → Launch |
| New Store Owner | Get Your First Sales | "How do I get sales?" | Marketing → Conversion → Retention |
| Established Store Owner | Build a Profitable Business | "How do I make this work consistently?" | Systems → Operations → Automation |
| Growing Business Owner | Grow Your Business | "How do I scale without losing control?" | Team → Advanced Growth → Expansion |
| Established Owner | Sell Your Business | "How do I maximize value?" | Readiness → Valuation → Exit Preparation |

This is why the site's Lifecycle page needs to support self-identification at any of the 5 stages, not assume every visitor starts at Start Your Business — see `docs/website.md`.

## Delivery Framework
Every engagement, regardless of which engine it primarily draws from, follows the same five-step loop:

1. **Diagnose**
2. **Recommend**
3. **Implement**
4. **Measure**
5. **Improve**

This is the same shape as the Intelligence → Decision → Implementation → Results → Optimization delivery model in `docs/strategy.md`, described here from the operational (engagement-level) rather than strategic (positioning-level) angle.

## Business Model
Merchantiva has six capabilities:

1. **Free Content** — YouTube, blog, newsletter, social content
2. **Academy** — courses, community, templates, training
3. **Intelligence** — research, data, reports
4. **Implementation** — stores, marketing systems, automation
5. **Growth & Automation** — scaling systems, optimization
6. **Exit** — value creation, sale preparation

**Important:** these are capabilities, not launch order. See `docs/jobs-to-be-done.md` — Current Commercial Focus — for what to prioritize commercially right now (Ecommerce Startup, Store Development, Growth, Marketing Automation), which draws on these six capabilities but isn't identical to them.
