# Positioning

## Needs-Based Positioning
Merchantiva follows Michael Porter's concept of needs-based positioning.

Instead of organizing the business around individual services, Merchantiva is organized around serving the complete and evolving needs of a specific customer segment: **ecommerce entrepreneurs**.

As entrepreneurs move through different stages of building a business, their needs change. Merchantiva evolves alongside them by providing the next knowledge, tools, and services they need to continue progressing.

## Target Customer
Merchantiva primarily serves aspiring and early-stage ecommerce entrepreneurs. These entrepreneurs typically:

- Want to start an ecommerce business
- Need help choosing what to sell
- Want to reduce risk before investing time and money
- Need guidance launching an online store
- Want to build and grow a profitable ecommerce business
- Eventually want to scale or sell their business

As Merchantiva expands, it continues serving the same customer throughout their entire business journey rather than acquiring an entirely different customer.

## Strategic Position
Merchantiva is the decision and implementation partner for ecommerce entrepreneurs.

Merchantiva helps entrepreneurs discover opportunities, validate ideas, launch online stores, grow profitable businesses, and prepare those businesses for acquisition by combining research, education, community, and implementation into one integrated ecosystem. Research informs a recommended action; Merchantiva then helps execute it, measure results, and improve. See `docs/strategy.md` — Merchantiva Delivery Model (Intelligence → Decision → Implementation → Results → Optimization).

Merchantiva's strategy is not to become another ecommerce agency or another online course platform. Its strategy is to become the trusted decision partner that helps entrepreneurs confidently navigate every stage of building an ecommerce business — and helps them act on that guidance, not just receive it.

**Tagline:** Better decisions. Better businesses.
