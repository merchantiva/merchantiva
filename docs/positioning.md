# Positioning

## Needs-Based Positioning
Merchantiva follows Michael Porter's concept of needs-based positioning.

Instead of organizing the business around individual services, Merchantiva is organized around serving the complete and evolving needs of a specific customer segment: **ecommerce entrepreneurs**.

As entrepreneurs move through different stages of building a business, their needs change. Merchantiva evolves alongside them by providing the next knowledge, tools, and services they need to continue progressing.

## Target Customer
Merchantiva primarily serves aspiring and early-stage ecommerce entrepreneurs. These entrepreneurs typically:

- Want to start an ecommerce business
- Need help choosing what to sell
- Want to reduce risk before investing time and money
- Need guidance launching an online store
- Want to build and grow a profitable ecommerce business
- Eventually want to scale or sell their business

As Merchantiva expands, it continues serving the same customer throughout their entire business journey rather than acquiring an entirely different customer.

## Strategic Position
Merchantiva is the long-term partner that helps ecommerce entrepreneurs make better decisions and accomplish the next important job — from starting an ecommerce business through growth and eventual sale.

Merchantiva helps entrepreneurs move through five stages — **Start Your Business → Get Your First Sales → Build a Profitable Business → Grow Your Business → Sell Your Business** (see `docs/lifecycle.md`) — by combining research, education, community, and implementation into one integrated ecosystem. Research informs a recommended action; Merchantiva then helps execute it, measure results, and improve. See `docs/strategy.md` — Merchantiva Delivery Model (Intelligence → Decision → Implementation → Results → Optimization).

Merchantiva's strategy is not to become another ecommerce agency, consulting firm, course company, research company, or Shopify agency. Those capabilities are components of the broader system — the differentiation is understanding the *sequence* of needs an entrepreneur experiences, not simply offering many services.

> **This paragraph is internal positioning strategy — it guides tone and product decisions, it is not customer-facing copy.** Never publish a "what we are not" section, a contrast-with-competitors section, or this kind of language as visible text on the site. The customer-facing site should read like a company helping an ecommerce entrepreneur, not like a strategy document. See `docs/website.md`'s Content Notes for the explicit build rule.

**Two positioning expressions — use together, not as alternatives:**
- **Decision-oriented:** Merchantiva helps ecommerce entrepreneurs make better decisions throughout the lifecycle of their business. *(describes the strategic value Merchantiva provides)*
- **JTBD-oriented:** Merchantiva helps ecommerce entrepreneurs accomplish the next important job throughout the lifecycle of their business. *(describes the customer's immediate need)*

**Core positioning line (use this for hero copy):**
Merchantiva helps ecommerce entrepreneurs figure out what to do next — and helps them do it.

**Taglines:**
- Better decisions. Better businesses. *(brand tagline — use in logo lockup, brand materials)*
- From ecommerce idea to business exit, Merchantiva helps you figure out what to do next — and helps you do it. *(longer version — use on About page or sales conversations where more context fits; use the shorter core positioning line above for the homepage hero)*

## Founder Story
Personal, credible, first-person. Not a corporate "About Us" statement — should feel like a real person explaining why they built Merchantiva, based on firsthand experience rather than a generic desire to start an ecommerce services company.

**Narrative basis:** Always interested in starting new businesses and coming up with new ideas — naturally creative, enjoys figuring out what could become a business. Learned that having the idea is only part of the challenge; turning an idea into a real business takes knowledge, execution, and often other people who are good at the things you don't do yourself or can't get done alone. Learned to build small teams and bring in people who could help with the parts not best suited to the founder — that's what made it possible to turn more ideas into actual businesses. That experience led directly to Merchantiva: wanting to help other ecommerce entrepreneurs do the same — figure out what to do, learn what they need to know, find the right resources and people, and turn ideas into real, growing businesses.
